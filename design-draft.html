01 — Foundations

One ink, one accent

06 — If you approve

What I'd change, concretely

Buttons become one component

Four treatments collapse into primary / secondary / quiet, plus the text link. The blue-fill CTA animation on the homepage stays — it becomes the primary button's signature move.

Every grey comes from the ramp

No more one-off rgba values. Warm greys instead of pure black on cream, which is the single change that reads most as "expensive".

Spacing snaps to 4px

Section padding, card padding and gaps all come from the scale. Rhythm is what makes a page feel considered rather than assembled.

One motion curve

Three durations, one easing family, transform and opacity only. Plus a reduced-motion fallback, which the site does not have today.

Focus states everywhere

Keyboard users currently get nothing. This is a real accessibility gap and a visible quality signal.

Cards lift instead of outline

Testimonials, work cards and the project pages get the raised surface and hairline treatment.
